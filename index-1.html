💧 How is your goal calculated?
AquaFit uses the standard formula: Weight (kg) × 35 ml = your daily water need.
For example, a 70 kg person needs approximately 2,450 ml (2.45 litres) per day.
Adjust for activity level, climate, and health conditions as needed.
